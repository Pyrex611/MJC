What We Do

Four disciplines, one standard of evidence

Each engagement is staffed by the department built for it, and reviewed by someone who had no hand in preparing it. Here's what that looks like in practice.

External Audit & Assurance

An opinion you can put in front of a bank, a board, or a regulator.

Independent review of financial statements against ISA standards, culminating in a formal audit opinion — tested through fieldwork, not assumed from a checklist.

Tax Planning & Dispute Resolution

Compliance that doesn't cost you more than the tax itself.

Planning, statutory filings, and representation in disputes with revenue authorities — handled by people who track how the rules actually get applied, not just how they read.

Advisory & Consulting

Risk and process work for the decisions that don't wait for year-end.

Risk management, financial advisory, and business process improvement for organisations navigating growth, restructuring, or a decision they'd rather stress-test first.

Client Accounting Services (CAS)

A back office you don't have to think about.

Bookkeeping, payroll administration, and periodic reporting for clients who'd rather run their business than run their ledgers.

How An Engagement Runs

Planning to sign-off

The same four stages govern every engagement, regardless of department — it's how a Forensic Audit and a routine statutory audit both end up defensible.

i.

Planning & Risk Assessment

Scope is set, materiality thresholds agreed, and risk areas identified before a single file is opened.

ii.

Fieldwork

Evidence is gathered — testing controls, tracing transactions, and documenting every finding as it's made.

iii.

Independent Review (EQCR)

A reviewer outside the engagement team checks the file against standards before any opinion is drafted.

iv.

Reporting & Sign-Off

Findings are delivered in writing, discussed with the client, and the file is closed and archived.

Before You Reach Out

Common questions

How long does a typical statutory audit take?+
For a mid-sized company with organised records, planning through sign-off typically runs six to ten weeks. Timelines extend for first-year audits, complex group structures, or where fieldwork surfaces issues that need deeper testing.
Do you work with businesses outside Abuja?+
Yes. Fieldwork is scheduled on-site where required, and most engagement coordination happens through the client portal regardless of location.
What happens after I submit a request through the client portal?+
A department head reviews it, typically within one business day, and confirms scope and timing. From there, your request and any documents stay visible in your account.
Is my file reviewed by more than one person?+
On assurance engagements, yes — every file passes through independent EQCR before an opinion is issued, by someone who wasn't part of the original engagement team.

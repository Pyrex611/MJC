How An Engagement Runs

Planning to sign-off

The same four stages govern every engagement, regardless of department — it's how a Forensic Audit and a routine statutory audit both end up defensible.

i.

Planning & Risk Assessment

Scope is set, materiality thresholds agreed, and risk areas identified before a single file is opened.

ii.

Fieldwork

Evidence is gathered — testing controls, tracing transactions, and documenting every finding as it's made.

iii.

Independent Review (EQCR)

A reviewer outside the engagement team checks the file against standards before any opinion is drafted.

iv.

Reporting & Sign-Off

Findings are delivered in writing, discussed with the client, and the file is closed and archived.

Before You Reach Out

Frequently asked questions

The most common questions we hear from prospective clients — ask us anything else directly.

What's the difference between assurance and advisory work?+
Assurance work — like an external audit — results in an independent, formal opinion on your financial statements. Advisory work is consultative: we help you plan, assess risk, or improve a process, without issuing an opinion. Many clients use both, run by separate teams to preserve independence.
How long does a typical statutory audit take?+
For a mid-sized company with organised records, planning through sign-off typically runs six to ten weeks. Timelines extend for first-year audits, complex group structures, or where fieldwork surfaces issues that need deeper testing.
Do you work with businesses outside Abuja?+
Yes. Fieldwork is scheduled on-site where required, and most engagement coordination — status updates, document requests, draft reviews — happens through the client portal regardless of location.
What happens after I submit a request through the client portal?+
A department head reviews it, typically within one business day, and confirms a scope and time. From there, your request, any documents exchanged, and status updates all stay visible under My Requests.
Is my information reviewed by more than one person?+
On assurance engagements, yes — every file passes through independent Engagement Quality Control Review (EQCR) before an opinion is issued, by someone who wasn't part of the original engagement team.
